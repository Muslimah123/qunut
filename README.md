# Qunūt Follow-Along

Read along with Sheikh Muhammad Jebril's dua al-qunūt. Each line shows the Arabic with full vowel marks, a transliteration for pronunciation and a plain English meaning. The line being recited lights up as the video plays, and tapping any line jumps the recitation to it.

The recitation plays from the [original video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PDHmIYFpWlk) on [the Sheikh's YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@Mohamed_Jebril). No audio or video is copied into this site.

## Notes

- Line times were aligned to the recording automatically and can be a second or two off. If the highlighting runs ahead of or behind the voice, use **Timing − / +** on the page; your setting is remembered in your browser.
- The English is a concise rendering to aid understanding while reciting, not an authoritative translation. Lines that quote the Qurʾān carry their sūrah and āyah.
- Vowel marks are reading aids; let the recitation guide rhythm and pronunciation.

The page is a single static file, `index.html`, with no build step.
