About this edition

Arabic with full vowel marks, a transliteration for pronunciation, and a plain English meaning, following the recording line by line. Repeated lines appear once with their count. Lines that quote the Qurʾān carry their sūrah and āyah; some quote part of a verse.

Times were aligned to the recording automatically and can be a second or two early or late. The English is a concise rendering to aid understanding while reciting, not an authoritative translation. Vowel marks are reading aids; let the recitation guide rhythm and pronunciation.

The recitation is by Sheikh Muhammad Jebril and plays from the original video on his channel; it is not copied here. If the highlighting runs ahead of or behind the voice, use Timing − / + beside the view options.
